Frequently Asked Questions

How do the 4 mascots collaborate during a task?

News Scout pulls empirical wire facts, Grok measures cultural sentiment, Dots performs high-level strategic reasoning, and Muse creates an actionable 48-hour checklist.

Where are my API keys stored?

Keys are stored strictly in your browser's private LocalStorage and transmitted over TLS 1.3 to your local FastAPI backend per mission. They never persist on remote servers.

Can I pin notes to guide future missions?

Yes! The Executive Scratchpad allows you to pin directives. Pinned notes are automatically injected into the squad's system context on every subsequent run.

How does the 3D Virtual Cyber-Office work?

Built with zero external dependencies using Three.js WebGL. Mascots physically walk to their desks when tasks are assigned, sit, type, and celebrate upon completion.
